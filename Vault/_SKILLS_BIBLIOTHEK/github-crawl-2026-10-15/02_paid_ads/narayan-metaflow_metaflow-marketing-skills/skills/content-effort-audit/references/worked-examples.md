2,800-word roundup restating vendor sites. Stock screenshots. Affiliate order. Generic “it depends.” No workflow was run. Swap the product names and the skeleton still stands.

**Adds (in order):** run one real workflow and date the screenshots; work pricing at two seat counts; name where the favorite tool loses.

Do not prescribe a longer bio. Do not prescribe more words.

## Site-level flag

Open three URLs. If they share a skeleton (same H2s, swapped entities, no new artifacts), escalate from page audit to production-system review. That is scaled low-effort, cousin of scaled content abuse — not a single rewrite ticket.
