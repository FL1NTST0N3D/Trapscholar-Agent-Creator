# Visible effort evidence rubric

Editorial instrument. Not claimed as Google’s weights. Score what is on the live URL.

## Signals (0–2 each, 0–12 total)

| Signal | 0 | 1 | 2 | Typical add when 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First-party information | All facts exist on other URLs | Some first-hand color | Claims only this creator could make | Measurement, teardown, primary doc, named workflow run |
| Non-replicable artifacts | Stock, scraped, templated | A few unique assets | Data, screenshots, methods, or tools others cannot copy cheaply | Dated screenshot, calculator, dataset, method card |
| Model-changing synthesis | Paraphrase of the SERP | Useful organization | The reader’s map of the topic changes | Decision rule, failure-mode matrix, disambiguation the SERP conflates |
| Craft vs purpose | Format fails the job | Adequate for the format | Talent/skill matched to page type | Match format to purpose (clip ≠ documentary) |
| Curation fingerprints | No choices visible | Some structure | Clear omissions, point of view, sequence | Cut a section, take a stance, order by job-to-be-done |
| Added value vs substitutes | Interchangeable with ranking pages | Small unique slice | The page does a job the rest of the SERP does not | A job similar pages do not do |

## Bands

| Total | Band | QRG-aligned language |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Lowest-risk evidence | Little to no effort, originality, and added value |
| 4–6 | Low | Some work, still substitutable |
| 7–9 | High | Satisfying human work is visible |
| 10–12 | Highest-evidence | Non-replicable residue; purpose-matched craft |

Apply a **purpose modifier** after the raw total. A 40-second clip and a long documentary are not supposed to land on the same raw bar. Informational and YMYL pages also need an accuracy pass — confident falsehoods are not high Page Quality even with artifacts.

## AI-assisted pages

- Generative AI alone does not determine effort or Page Quality.
- Score the residue, not the generator.
- Tone edits on a SERP paraphrase stay 0–3.
- AI that drafts around first-party artifacts can score High if those artifacts are on the page.
- Scaled publishing of near-duplicates is a site-level flag, not a single-URL rewrite ticket.

## What not to score

Hours, cost, outline decks, “human in the loop” checkboxes, byline prestige (that is E-E-A-T), word count, uniqueness of wording for the same facts (originality ≠ effort).
