# YouTube Ads Glossary

## Ad Formats

- **Skippable In-Stream:** Video ad that plays before, during, or after a video. Viewer can skip after 5 seconds. Charged per view (CPV) when viewer watches 30 seconds or interacts.
- **Non-Skippable In-Stream:** 15-second video ad that cannot be skipped. Charged per 1,000 impressions (CPM).
- **Bumper Ad:** 6-second non-skippable video ad. Charged per CPM. Designed for reach and frequency.
- **Shorts Ad:** Vertical (9:16) video ad appearing between organic Shorts. 15-60 seconds. Charged per CPM.
- **In-Feed Ad (Discovery):** Thumbnail + text ad appearing in YouTube search results, watch next, and Discover feed. Charged per click (CPC).
- **Demand Gen:** Multi-format campaign type that spans YouTube, Gmail, and Discover. Optimizes for conversions or ROAS.

## Metrics

- **View Rate:** Percentage of impressions that resulted in a view. View = 30 seconds watched or full ad if shorter.
- **CPV (Cost Per View):** Average cost per view. Only applicable to skippable in-stream ads.
- **CPM (Cost Per Mille):** Cost per 1,000 impressions. Used for bumper, non-skippable, and Shorts ads.
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Percentage of impressions that resulted in a click on the ad or CTA.
- **VTR (View-Through Rate):** Same as view rate.
- **VTC (View-Through Conversion):** A conversion that occurs after a user views (but does not click) an ad. YouTube's most important conversion metric.
- **Quartile Metrics:** Percentage of viewers who watched 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% of the video.
- **Watch Time:** Total hours of video ad content watched across all viewers.
- **Earned Views:** Views, likes, subscribers, and shares gained organically after seeing the ad.
- **Brand Lift:** Measured increase in brand awareness, ad recall, or consideration from ad exposure (requires Google Brand Lift study).

## Audience Types

- **Custom Intent:** Audience based on recent Google Search queries. Most powerful YouTube audience.
- **Affinity:** Broad interest-based audience from long-term behavior. Best for awareness.
- **In-Market:** Users actively researching products in a category. High purchase intent.
- **Life Events:** Users experiencing major life transitions (moving, graduating, getting married).
- **Custom Segments:** Audiences built from URLs, apps, and keywords you define.
- **Similar Audiences:** Users similar to your remarketing or customer lists.
- **Customer Match:** Your CRM email list uploaded to Google Ads for direct targeting.
- **RLSA (Remarketing Lists for Search Ads):** Using YouTube viewer lists to adjust search ad bids.

## Bidding

- **tCPV (Target CPV):** Automated bidding that targets an average cost per view.
- **tCPM (Target CPM):** Automated bidding targeting an average cost per 1,000 impressions.
- **Max Conversions:** Automated bidding to maximize conversions within budget. No CPA target.
- **tCPA (Target CPA):** Automated bidding targeting a specific cost per conversion. Requires 30+ conversions/month.
- **tROAS (Target ROAS):** Automated bidding targeting a return on ad spend. Requires 50+ conversions/month with revenue values.
- **Manual CPV:** Manual bid for each view. Maximum control, minimum automation.

## Campaign Settings

- **Frequency Cap:** Maximum number of times one user sees your ad in a given period.
- **Inventory Type:** Controls content safety level (Expanded, Standard, Limited).
- **Content Exclusions:** Specific content categories excluded from ad placement.
- **Ad Scheduling (Dayparting):** Running ads only during specific hours or days.
- **Device Targeting:** Selecting which devices (mobile, desktop, tablet, TV) see ads.
- **Video Ad Sequencing:** Showing ads in a specific order to the same user.

## Creative

- **ABCD Framework:** Google's creative framework — Attract, Brand, Connect, Direct.
- **Hook:** The first 5 seconds of a video ad. Critical for skippable ads.
- **End Card:** The final screen of a video ad showing CTA, logo, and key message.
- **Companion Banner:** 300x60 display ad shown alongside a video ad on desktop.
- **Sonic Branding:** Audio brand identity (jingle, sound logo) for recall.
- **UGC (User-Generated Content):** Content created by real users/customers. High authenticity.
