## Framework Selection Guide

### By Channel
- **Google Ads (RSA)**: AIDA, PAS, FAB — short, punchy, benefit-driven
- **Meta/Social Ads**: PAS, BAB, SSS — emotional, visual, scroll-stopping
- **Email Subject Lines**: Power Words, Headlines — curiosity + urgency
- **Email Body**: AIDA, PAS, PASTOR — narrative flow to CTA
- **Landing Pages**: AIDA, PASTOR, StoryBrand — full persuasion arc
- **Sales Pages**: PASTOR, QUEST, StoryBrand — long-form persuasion
- **Social Media Posts**: SSS, Star-Chain-Hook, BAB — quick emotional hit

### By Objective
- **Lead Generation**: PAS + CTA formulas + objection handling
- **E-commerce Sales**: FAB + social proof + urgency power words
- **SaaS Signups**: AIDA + 4Cs + CTA formulas
- **Brand Awareness**: StoryBrand + storytelling + social proof
- **Retargeting**: BAB + objection handling + social proof

### By Audience Temperature
- **Cold Traffic**: PAS, BAB, headlines — establish problem, build trust
- **Warm Traffic**: AIDA, FAB, social proof — reinforce value, build desire
- **Hot Traffic**: CTA formulas, objection handling, urgency — close the deal

## Universal Copy Rules

1. **One idea per sentence.** Clarity beats cleverness.
2. **Write to one person.** "You" > "customers" > "people."
3. **Benefits over features.** What does it DO for them?
4. **Specific beats vague.** "37% increase" > "significant improvement."
5. **Short paragraphs.** 1-3 sentences max. White space is your friend.
6. **Active voice.** "We built" > "It was built by us."
7. **Cut adverbs.** "Skyrocketed" > "grew very quickly."
8. **Read it aloud.** If you stumble, rewrite.
9. **One CTA per piece.** Multiple CTAs = no CTA.
10. **Test everything.** Your opinion is not data.

## Tone & Voice Modifiers

When generating copy, apply these modifiers:

| Modifier | Effect | Example |
|----------|--------|---------|
| `--formal` | Professional, corporate | "Optimize your operational efficiency" |
| `--casual` | Conversational, friendly | "Make your work life way easier" |
| `--urgent` | Time-pressure, scarcity | "Only 3 spots left. Doors close Friday." |
| `--luxury` | Premium, exclusive | "For the discerning few who demand excellence" |
| `--technical` | Detailed, spec-driven | "256-bit AES encryption with zero-knowledge architecture" |
| `--playful` | Fun, witty | "Your spreadsheets called. They want a divorce." |
| `--empathetic` | Understanding, caring | "We know how frustrating it is when..." |
| `--bold` | Provocative, challenging | "Everything you've been told about SEO is wrong." |
