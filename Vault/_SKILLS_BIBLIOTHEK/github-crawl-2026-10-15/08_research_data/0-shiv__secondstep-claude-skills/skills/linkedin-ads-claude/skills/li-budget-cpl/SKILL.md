# Budget & CPL Sub-Skill

## Purpose

Audit and optimize LinkedIn Ads budget allocation, CPL performance against industry benchmarks, bid strategy configuration, and funnel-stage budget distribution. Recommend reallocation for maximum ROI and pipeline efficiency.

## LinkedIn Ads Budget Fundamentals

### Minimum Budget Requirements

LinkedIn requires meaningful daily budgets to generate results. Unlike Google or Meta, LinkedIn audiences are smaller and CPCs are higher, so minimum thresholds matter more.

| Budget Level | Daily Amount | Monthly Amount | Expected Results |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Absolute Minimum | $10/day | ~$300/mo | Not recommended -- too slow for learning |
| Functional Minimum | $50/day | ~$1,500/mo | Single campaign, narrow audience |
| Recommended Start | $100/day | ~$3,000/mo | 2-3 campaigns, basic funnel |
| Growth Mode | $250/day | ~$7,500/mo | Full funnel, multiple audiences |
| Scale Mode | $500+/day | ~$15,000+/mo | ABM + prospecting, aggressive testing |

**LinkedIn Learning Phase:**
- LinkedIn needs approximately 15 conversions per week per campaign to optimize delivery
- At $100 CPL, that requires $1,500/week ($6,000/month) per campaign
- Running too many campaigns on small budgets means none exit learning phase

## Budget Allocation Framework

### Funnel-Stage Budget Split

**For New LinkedIn Accounts (First 90 Days):**
```
TOFU: 30% -- Build awareness and feed the funnel
MOFU: 50% -- Content downloads, engagement, list building
BOFU: 20% -- Demo requests, limited retargeting pool initially
```

**For Established Accounts (90+ Days):**
```
TOFU: 20% -- Maintain awareness, refresh audiences
MOFU: 50% -- Primary pipeline driver
BOFU: 30% -- Expanded retargeting pools, ABM
```

**For ABM-Heavy Accounts:**
```
TOFU: 15% -- Targeted awareness for named accounts
MOFU: 35% -- Account-specific content
BOFU: 50% -- Direct engagement, InMail, demos
```

### Budget Allocation by Audience Type

| Audience Type | Budget Share | Rationale |
|--------------|-------------|-----------|
| Prospecting (cold) | 50-60% | Growth engine, feeds retargeting |
| Retargeting (warm) | 25-35% | Highest conversion rate |
| ABM (named accounts) | 10-20% | Highest value, smallest audience |

## Bid Strategy Guide

### Available Bid Strategies

| Strategy | How It Works | Best For | Risk |
|----------|-------------|----------|------|
| Maximum Delivery | LinkedIn auto-bids to spend full budget | Beginners, awareness campaigns | Overpaying for clicks |
| Target Cost | Set target CPL, LinkedIn optimizes around it | Predictable CPL goals | Underdelivery if target too low |
| Manual CPC | Set max CPC bid manually | Cost control, experienced advertisers | Requires active management |
| Manual CPM | Set max CPM bid manually | Brand awareness, high-CTR ads | Volume risk |
| Enhanced CPC | Manual CPC with auto-adjustments | Balanced control + optimization | Slight cost variability |

**Recommendation by Funnel Stage:**
- **TOFU:** Maximum Delivery (prioritize reach) or Manual CPM (cost control)
- **MOFU:** Target Cost (predictable CPL) or Enhanced CPC (balanced)
- **BOFU:** Manual CPC (tight cost control) or Target Cost (if volume sufficient)

## Audit Checklist

1. **Daily Budget Adequacy** (3 pts)
   - Is each campaign spending at least $50/day?
   - Is total account spend sufficient for the number of active campaigns?
   - Are campaigns able to exit the learning phase (15+ conversions/week)?

2. **CPL vs Industry Benchmark** (4 pts)
   - Is CPL within the industry benchmark range?
   - Is CPL trending down over the last 30/60/90 days?
   - Are there campaigns with CPL 2x+ above benchmark (red flag)?
   - Is CPL segmented by funnel stage (not blended)?

3. **Budget Allocation Across Funnel** (4 pts)
   - Is budget distributed across TOFU/MOFU/BOFU (not all in one stage)?
   - Does MOFU receive 40-60% of total budget?
   - Is BOFU retargeting adequately funded?
   - Is there an ABM-specific budget allocation?

4. **Bid Strategy Configuration** (4 pts)
   - Is the bid strategy appropriate for each campaign's objective?
   - Are manual bids set within competitive range (not too low for delivery, not too high for waste)?
   - Are bid adjustments being made based on performance data?
   - Is there a clear bid strategy rationale documented?

## Common Budget & CPL Mistakes

1. **Budget spread too thin** -- 10 campaigns at $15/day each = none exits learning phase
2. **No funnel allocation** -- 100% budget on BOFU demo requests with no awareness or nurture
3. **Blended CPL reporting** -- mixing TOFU CPL ($40) with BOFU CPL ($300) into one "average CPL" that means nothing
4. **Maximum Delivery on BOFU** -- auto-bidding overpays for high-intent clicks; use manual CPC or Target Cost
5. **Not accounting for LinkedIn's premium** -- comparing LinkedIn CPL to Google/Meta CPL without considering lead quality differential
6. **Ignoring cost per MQL** -- a $200 CPL that converts to MQL at 50% = $400 cost per MQL; a $100 CPL that converts at 10% = $1,000 cost per MQL
7. **No seasonal adjustment** -- LinkedIn CPMs spike in Q4 and January; budgets should flex accordingly
8. **Forgetting LinkedIn's 48-hour ramp** -- new campaigns or major changes trigger a learning period; do not judge performance before day 3-5
