# Content Formats Sub-Skill

## Purpose

Audit and optimize LinkedIn ad format selection and creative strategy. Evaluate format-to-funnel alignment, creative diversity, performance benchmarks, and refresh cadence. Recommend format experiments based on campaign objectives.

## LinkedIn Ad Format Deep Dive

### Single Image Ads

**Overview:** The workhorse of LinkedIn advertising. A single static image with headline, intro text, and CTA. Available across all objectives.

**Specifications:**
- Image size: 1200x627px (landscape) or 1080x1080px (square)
- Intro text: up to 600 characters (first 150 visible before "see more")
- Headline: up to 200 characters (70 recommended)
- File types: JPG, PNG, GIF (static)
- Max file size: 5MB

**Performance Benchmarks:**
| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| CTR | 0.4% - 0.7% |
| CPC | $3 - $7 |
| CPL (with LGF) | $75 - $200 |
| Engagement Rate | 0.5% - 1.0% |

**Best Practices:**
- Use bold, contrasting colors that stand out in the LinkedIn feed (avoid LinkedIn blue #0077B5)
- Include a clear value proposition in the image itself (many users see the image first)
- Square format (1080x1080) gets 15-20% more feed real estate on mobile
- Keep intro text concise -- front-load the hook in the first 150 characters
- Test person images vs graphic illustrations vs data visualizations

**Best For:** Lead generation, content promotion, direct response. Lowest CPL at scale.

### Carousel Ads

**Overview:** Multiple cards (2-10) that users swipe through. Each card has its own image, headline, and destination URL.

**Specifications:**
- Cards: 2-10 per carousel
- Card image: 1080x1080px (square only)
- Card headline: up to 45 characters
- Intro text: up to 255 characters
- File types: JPG, PNG
- Max file size: 10MB per card

**Performance Benchmarks:**
| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| CTR | 0.8% - 1.4% (2x single image) |
| CPC | $4 - $8 |
| Engagement Rate | 1.0% - 2.0% |
| Swipe Rate | 30% - 50% (cards 2-5) |

**Best Practices:**
- Tell a story across cards -- create a narrative that compels swiping
- Use the first card as a hook with a compelling question or statistic
- Limit to 5-7 cards (engagement drops significantly after card 5)
- Use the last card as a strong CTA
- Each card should be valuable standalone, not just a fragment

**Best For:** Product features, step-by-step guides, case study highlights, comparison frameworks.

### Video Ads

**Overview:** Native video content that auto-plays (muted) in the LinkedIn feed. Supports all campaign objectives.

**Specifications:**
- Duration: 3 seconds to 30 minutes (15-30s optimal for awareness, 60-90s for consideration)
- Aspect ratios: 16:9 (landscape), 1:1 (square), 9:16 (vertical)
- Resolution: 360p to 1080p (1080p recommended)
- File types: MP4
- Max file size: 200MB
- Captions: Required (85% watch with sound off)

**Performance Benchmarks:**
| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| View Rate (2s) | 25% - 35% |
| View Rate (25%) | 15% - 25% |
| View Rate (50%) | 8% - 15% |
| Completion Rate | 5% - 12% |
| CPV | $0.05 - $0.15 |
| Engagement | 5x higher than static |

**Best Practices:**
- Hook in first 3 seconds (before auto-play stops)
- Always add captions/subtitles -- 85% watch without sound
- Square or vertical format for mobile-first consumption
- Include brand name and logo within first 5 seconds
- End with a clear CTA frame
- Use customer testimonials for highest credibility

**Best For:** Brand awareness, product demos, customer testimonials, thought leadership.

### Document Ads

**Overview:** Native document viewer (PDF, DOC, PPT) that opens in-feed. Users can preview content before gating with a Lead Gen Form. The standout LinkedIn-specific format.

**Specifications:**
- File types: PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, PPTX
- Max pages: 300 (10-15 recommended)
- Max file size: 100MB
- Preview pages: Set how many pages are free before gating
- Gate: Optional Lead Gen Form after preview pages

**Performance Benchmarks:**
| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| Form Completion Rate | 22.73% (highest of all formats) |
| Engagement Rate | 2.0% - 4.0% |
| CPL (gated) | $50 - $150 |
| Pages Viewed (avg) | 3-5 |

**Best Practices:**
- Gate after 3-5 preview pages -- enough to demonstrate value but leave them wanting more
- Design for mobile viewing (large fonts, minimal text per page)
- Use the first page as a compelling cover with clear topic and benefit
- Include data visualizations and charts (LinkedIn audience loves data)
- End ungated preview on a cliffhanger or key insight

**Best For:** Whitepapers, benchmarks, research reports, playbooks, case studies. THE format for MOFU lead generation on LinkedIn.

### Message Ads (InMail)

**Overview:** Sponsored messages delivered directly to a member's LinkedIn inbox. One message per member per 45 days (frequency cap).

**Specifications:**
- Subject line: up to 60 characters
- Body: up to 1,500 characters
- CTA button: up to 20 characters
- Banner image: 300x250px (optional, desktop only)
- Sender: Must be a LinkedIn member (not a company page)

**Performance Benchmarks:**
| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| Open Rate | 50% - 60% |
| CTR | 3% - 5% |
| CPC | $20 - $50 |
| CPL | $100 - $300 |

**Best Practices:**
- Use a real person as sender (not a company), ideally a recognizable executive
- Keep subject line short and personalized
- Write in first person, conversational tone
- One clear CTA -- do not give multiple options
- Time delivery for Tuesday-Thursday business hours

**Best For:** High-value BOFU offers (demos, consultations), event invitations, ABM outreach.

### Conversation Ads

**Overview:** Choose-your-own-adventure messages with multiple CTA buttons that create branching dialogue flows.

**Specifications:**
- Multiple CTA buttons per message (up to 5)
- Branching logic with follow-up messages per CTA
- Banner image: 300x250px (optional)
- Sender: Must be a LinkedIn member

**Performance Benchmarks:**
| Metric | Benchmark |
|--------|-----------|
| Open Rate | 50%+ |
| Engagement Rate | 4x email |
| CTR | 5% - 10% |
| CPL | $80 - $200 |

**Best For:** Multi-offer campaigns, qualification flows, event registration with follow-up.

## Format-to-Funnel Alignment Matrix

| Funnel Stage | Primary Format | Secondary Format | Avoid |
|-------------|---------------|-----------------|-------|
| TOFU | Video, Single Image | Carousel | Message Ads |
| MOFU | Document Ads, Carousel | Video, Conversation | Text Ads |
| BOFU | Lead Gen Form (Single Image), Message | Conversation Ads | Video (alone) |

## Audit Checklist

1. **Format Diversity** (3 pts)
   - Is the account using at least 3 different ad formats?
   - Are format selections appropriate for each campaign objective?
   - Has the account tested Document Ads (LinkedIn's unique advantage)?

2. **Format-Funnel Alignment** (3 pts)
   - Are TOFU campaigns using awareness-optimized formats (Video, branded imagery)?
   - Are MOFU campaigns using engagement formats (Document, Carousel)?
   - Are BOFU campaigns using conversion formats (LGF, Message)?

3. **Creative Quality & Volume** (2 pts)
   - Does each campaign have at least 2 active ads for testing?
   - Are creatives visually distinct (not minor copy variations)?

4. **Refresh Cadence** (2 pts)
   - Are ads refreshed every 4-6 weeks to combat creative fatigue?
   - Are underperforming ads paused and replaced (not left running)?

## Scoring

| Score | Rating | Meaning |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 9-10 | Elite | Diverse formats, perfect funnel alignment, regular testing |
| 7-8 | Strong | Good variety with minor alignment gaps |
| 5-6 | Average | Limited formats or poor funnel matching |
| 3-4 | Weak | Only 1-2 formats, no testing structure |
| 0-2 | Critical | Wrong formats for objectives, stale creative |

## Common Format Mistakes

1. **Only using Single Image** -- missing Document Ads' 22.73% completion rate advantage
2. **Video without captions** -- 85% of LinkedIn videos are watched muted
3. **Carousel with 10 cards** -- engagement drops sharply after card 5
4. **Message Ads for TOFU** -- feels intrusive for cold audiences, burns goodwill
5. **No creative refresh** -- same ads running for 3+ months = declining CTR and rising costs
6. **One ad per campaign** -- no A/B testing data, cannot optimize
7. **Ignoring Document Ads** -- LinkedIn's most unique and highest-performing format for B2B lead gen
