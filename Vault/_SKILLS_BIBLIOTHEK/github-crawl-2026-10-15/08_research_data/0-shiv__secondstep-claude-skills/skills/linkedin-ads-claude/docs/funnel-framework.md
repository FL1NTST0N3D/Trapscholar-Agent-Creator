# Full-Funnel B2B Framework for LinkedIn Ads

## Overview

The full-funnel approach ensures LinkedIn advertising moves prospects through awareness, consideration, and decision stages with coordinated messaging, formats, and retargeting. This framework is designed for B2B companies selling to enterprise and mid-market buyers.

## Why Full-Funnel Matters on LinkedIn

Most B2B companies make the mistake of running only BOFU (demo request) campaigns on LinkedIn. This fails because:

1. **Cold audiences do not convert to demos** -- enterprise buyers need 7-13 touchpoints before engaging sales
2. **BOFU-only creates CPL death spirals** -- as retargeting pools deplete, CPL climbs indefinitely
3. **No brand equity** -- prospects who have never heard of you ignore demo request ads
4. **Missing the 95/5 rule** -- only 5% of B2B buyers are in-market at any time; 95% need nurturing

A full-funnel approach builds demand (not just captures it), creating sustainable pipeline growth.

## The Three-Stage Framework

### Stage 1: TOFU -- Build Awareness & Trust

**Goal:** Make your brand known and trusted within your ICP.

**Objective:** Brand Awareness or Video Views

**Content Strategy:**
- Thought leadership from founders/executives
- Industry trend reports and data insights
- Short educational videos (15-30 seconds)
- Ungated blog posts and infographics
- Company culture and values content

**Targeting:**
- Broadest ICP targeting: Industry + Seniority (audience 100K-500K+)
- Audience Expansion: ON
- Exclusions: Existing customers, employees, competitors

**Ad Formats:**
- Video Ads (brand story, product explainer)
- Single Image (thought leadership, data stats)
- Document Ads (ungated educational content)

**Budget:** 20-30% of total LinkedIn spend

**KPIs:**
| Metric | Benchmark | Goal |
|--------|-----------|------|
| Impressions | - | Maximize reach |
| CPM | $6-$12 | Cost-efficient awareness |
| Video View Rate | 25-35% | Content relevance |
| Engagement Rate | >0.5% | Message resonance |
| Frequency | 2-4x/month | Sufficient exposure |

**Retargeting Output:** Video viewers (25%+), post engagers, company page visitors feed into MOFU audiences.

### Stage 2: MOFU -- Engage & Educate

**Goal:** Convert aware prospects into engaged leads with valuable content.

**Objective:** Lead Generation or Engagement

**Content Strategy:**
- Gated whitepapers and research reports
- Case studies with quantified results
- Webinar registrations
- Product comparison guides
- ROI calculators and assessment tools
- Benchmark reports

**Targeting:**
- TOFU retargeting audiences (video viewers, engagers)
- Refined prospecting: Industry + Size + Seniority + Function (audience 25K-150K)
- Audience Expansion: OFF
- Exclusions: Existing customers, TOFU exclusion overlap

**Ad Formats:**
- Document Ads + Lead Gen Form (22.73% completion -- the MOFU hero)
- Carousel Ads (case study highlights, feature breakdowns)
- Video Ads (customer testimonials, 60-90 seconds)
- Conversation Ads (multi-CTA content journeys)

**Budget:** 50-60% of total LinkedIn spend (primary investment stage)

**KPIs:**
| Metric | Benchmark | Goal |
|--------|-----------|------|
| CTR | 0.5-0.8% | Content interest |
| CPC | $3-$7 | Efficient clicks |
| CPL | $75-$200 | Cost-effective leads |
| LGF Completion | >13% | Form optimization |
| Content Downloads | - | Lead volume |

**Retargeting Output:** Lead Gen Form openers (non-submitters), content downloaders, website visitors feed into BOFU audiences.

### Stage 3: BOFU -- Convert & Close

**Goal:** Convert high-intent prospects into sales conversations.

**Objective:** Lead Generation or Website Conversions

**Content Strategy:**
- Demo request offers
- Free trial or freemium access
- Consultation booking
- Custom pricing / ROI analysis
- Competitive comparison (why choose us)

**Targeting:**
- MOFU retargeting audiences (LGF openers, content downloaders)
- Website visitors (pricing page, product pages, 30-day window)
- ABM company name targeting (Tier 1 accounts)
- Audience Expansion: OFF (always)
- Exclusions: Existing customers, recent converters (30 days)

**Ad Formats:**
- Single Image + Lead Gen Form (lowest CPL at scale)
- Message Ads (personalized 1:1 outreach, 50%+ open rate)
- Conversation Ads (qualification flow before demo)
- Single Image + Landing Page (for high-quality leads)

**Budget:** 15-25% of total LinkedIn spend

**KPIs:**
| Metric | Benchmark | Goal |
|--------|-----------|------|
| CPL | $150-$600 | Quality leads |
| Conversion Rate | 2-5% | Landing page / form |
| Lead-to-MQL Rate | 30-50% | Lead quality |
| Cost per MQL | $300-$1,200 | Pipeline efficiency |
| Demo Show Rate | 60-80% | Lead intent |

## Retargeting Chain Architecture

```
TOFU Audiences (cold)
  |
  | Video viewers (25%+), post engagers, page visitors
  v
MOFU Audiences (warm)
  |
  | LGF openers, content downloaders, website visitors
  v
BOFU Audiences (hot)
  |
  | Demo requesters, trial starters, pricing page visitors
  v
CUSTOMER (exclude from acquisition, target for upsell/cross-sell)
```

**Key Rule:** Each stage's output becomes the next stage's input. Without this chain, you are running disconnected campaigns, not a funnel.

## Budget Allocation by Company Stage

| Company Stage | TOFU | MOFU | BOFU | Total Monthly |
|--------------|------|------|------|--------------|
| Pre-launch / New brand | 40% | 40% | 20% | $3,000-$5,000 |
| Growth (0-$1M ARR) | 30% | 50% | 20% | $5,000-$10,000 |
| Scaling ($1-10M ARR) | 25% | 50% | 25% | $10,000-$30,000 |
| Enterprise ($10M+ ARR) | 20% | 45% | 35% | $30,000+ |

## Timeline: First 90 Days

### Month 1: Foundation
- Set up Insight Tag and conversion events
- Create first campaign group per funnel stage
- Launch 1-2 TOFU campaigns (video + single image)
- Launch 1-2 MOFU campaigns (document ads + carousel)
- Upload customer exclusion list
- Build initial retargeting audiences

### Month 2: Optimize & Expand
- Review Month 1 data, pause underperformers
- Launch BOFU campaigns (retargeting pools should be 1,000+)
- A/B test ad creative across all stages
- Upload ABM company list for Tier 1 targeting
- Refresh creative on TOFU campaigns
- Set up CRM integration for Lead Gen Forms

### Month 3: Scale & Refine
- Increase budget on winning campaigns
- Launch lookalike audiences from customer list
- Test Conversation Ads or Message Ads for BOFU
- Begin offline conversion uploads from CRM
- Implement CAPI for improved tracking
- Generate first full audit report

## Common Full-Funnel Mistakes

1. **Skipping TOFU** -- jumping straight to demos with cold audiences
2. **No retargeting chain** -- each funnel stage runs independently
3. **Budget imbalance** -- 90% on BOFU, 10% on everything else
4. **Same creative everywhere** -- awareness ads looking like conversion ads
5. **No exclusions between stages** -- BOFU prospects seeing TOFU content
6. **Measuring TOFU by CPL** -- TOFU is measured by reach, frequency, and engagement, not cost per lead
7. **Giving up too early** -- LinkedIn full-funnel takes 60-90 days to show pipeline impact
