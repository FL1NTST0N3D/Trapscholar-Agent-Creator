# LinkedIn Ads Glossary

## Platform Terms

**Campaign Manager**: LinkedIn's self-serve advertising platform for creating, managing, and reporting on ad campaigns.

**Campaign Group**: The top-level organizational container in Campaign Manager. Contains one or more campaigns. Used to set overall budget caps, scheduling, and status.

**Campaign**: A set of ads sharing the same objective, targeting, format, bid strategy, and budget. The core operational unit.

**Ad**: An individual creative (image, video, document, or message) within a campaign. Multiple ads per campaign enable A/B testing.

**Insight Tag**: A lightweight JavaScript snippet installed on your website that enables conversion tracking, website retargeting, and website demographics.

**LinkedIn Audience Network (LAN)**: LinkedIn's network of third-party apps and websites where LinkedIn ads can be shown beyond the LinkedIn feed.

## Objectives

**Brand Awareness**: Maximize impressions. Charged on CPM basis. Best for TOFU.

**Website Visits**: Drive clicks to your website. Charged on CPC basis.

**Engagement**: Maximize social actions (likes, comments, shares, follows). Charged on CPC or CPM.

**Video Views**: Maximize video plays. Charged on CPV (cost per view).

**Lead Generation**: Collect leads via LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms (native, in-feed forms). Charged on CPC or CPM.

**Website Conversions**: Drive specific actions on your website (form fills, purchases). Requires Insight Tag with conversion events. Charged on CPC or CPM.

**Job Applicants**: Drive job applications. Specialized objective for recruitment.

## Targeting Terms

**Matched Audiences**: Custom audience segments built from your own data -- website visitors, contact lists, company lists, or engagement audiences.

**Lookalike Audiences**: LinkedIn-generated audiences that share professional attributes with your source audience (customers, leads, website visitors).

**Audience Expansion**: A campaign setting that allows LinkedIn to automatically expand your targeting to include similar members outside your defined audience. On by default.

**ABM (Account-Based Marketing)**: A strategy that targets specific named companies rather than broad firmographic criteria. Executed via company name targeting or company list uploads.

**ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)**: The description of a company that would be your best customer -- industry, size, revenue, geography, tech stack.

**Buyer Persona**: The description of an individual decision-maker within your ICP -- job title, seniority, function, pain points.

## Ad Format Terms

**Sponsored Content**: Ads that appear natively in the LinkedIn feed. Includes Single Image, Carousel, Video, Document, and Event formats.

**Document Ads**: A LinkedIn-specific format that displays a PDF/PPT/DOC in a native viewer within the feed. Can be gated with a Lead Gen Form after a set number of preview pages.

**Lead Gen Form (LGF)**: A native LinkedIn form that appears when a user clicks a CTA on a Sponsored Content ad. Fields are pre-filled from the user's LinkedIn profile, reducing friction.

**Message Ads (InMail)**: Sponsored messages delivered to a user's LinkedIn inbox. One message per user per 45 days (frequency cap). Requires a real LinkedIn member as sender.

**Conversation Ads**: Similar to Message Ads but with multiple CTA buttons that create branching conversation flows. Enables choose-your-own-adventure style interactions.

**Carousel Ads**: Multi-card ads (2-10 cards) that users swipe through horizontally. Each card has its own image, headline, and optional destination URL.

**Text Ads**: Simple text-based ads shown in the right rail or top banner of LinkedIn desktop. Lowest cost, limited placement.

**Dynamic Ads**: Personalized ads that automatically insert the viewer's profile photo, name, or company. Used for follower growth and content promotion.

## Metrics & Measurement

**CTR (Click-Through Rate)**: Clicks divided by impressions. LinkedIn average for Sponsored Content: ~0.5%.

**CPC (Cost Per Click)**: Total spend divided by total clicks. LinkedIn range: $3-$7 for Sponsored Content.

**CPM (Cost Per Mille)**: Cost per 1,000 impressions. LinkedIn range: $6-$12 for Sponsored Content.

**CPL (Cost Per Lead)**: Total spend divided by total leads. Varies widely by industry ($75-$600).

**CPV (Cost Per View)**: Cost per video view (2+ seconds). LinkedIn range: $0.05-$0.15.

**MQL (Marketing Qualified Lead)**: A lead that meets predefined qualification criteria (budget, authority, need, timeline) and is ready for sales follow-up.

**SQL (Sales Qualified Lead)**: An MQL that sales has accepted and is actively pursuing as an opportunity.

**ROAS (Return on Ad Spend)**: Revenue generated divided by ad spend. LinkedIn B2B SaaS average: 113%.

**LTV (Lifetime Value)**: The total revenue a customer generates over their entire relationship. Used to justify higher LinkedIn CPLs.

**Conversion Rate**: Percentage of clicks or form views that result in a conversion (form submission, purchase, etc.).

## Bidding Terms

**Maximum Delivery**: LinkedIn auto-bids to spend your full daily budget. Simplest option, least control.

**Target Cost**: You set a target CPL or CPC, and LinkedIn optimizes bids to hit that target on average.

**Manual CPC**: You set the maximum CPC you are willing to pay. Most control, requires active management.

**Manual CPM**: You set the maximum CPM you are willing to pay. Best for high-CTR ads.

**Enhanced CPC**: Manual CPC with LinkedIn's automatic bid adjustments. Balanced control + optimization.

**Learning Phase**: The initial period (typically 7-14 days) when LinkedIn's algorithm is calibrating delivery and bidding. Performance is volatile during this phase. Need ~15 conversions/week to exit.

## Tracking & Attribution

**CAPI (Conversions API)**: Server-side conversion tracking that sends data directly from your server to LinkedIn, bypassing browser limitations.

**Click-Through Attribution**: Conversion credited to a campaign if the user clicked the ad before converting. Default window: 30 days.

**View-Through Attribution**: Conversion credited to a campaign if the user saw (but did not click) the ad before converting. Default window: 7 days.

**UTM Parameters**: URL tracking parameters (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content) appended to destination URLs for analytics attribution.

**Offline Conversions**: CRM data (MQLs, SQLs, closed deals) uploaded back to LinkedIn to measure downstream advertising impact.

## Funnel Terms

**TOFU (Top of Funnel)**: Awareness stage. Goal: reach and educate target audience. Metrics: impressions, video views, engagement.

**MOFU (Middle of Funnel)**: Consideration stage. Goal: engage and capture leads. Metrics: content downloads, webinar registrations, CTR.

**BOFU (Bottom of Funnel)**: Decision stage. Goal: convert high-intent prospects. Metrics: demo requests, trial starts, CPL, conversion rate.

**Full-Funnel**: A campaign strategy that runs coordinated campaigns across all three funnel stages, with retargeting chains connecting them.
