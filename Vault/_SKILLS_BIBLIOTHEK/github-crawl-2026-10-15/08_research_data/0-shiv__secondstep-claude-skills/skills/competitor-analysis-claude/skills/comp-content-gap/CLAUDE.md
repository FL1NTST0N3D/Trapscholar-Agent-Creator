# Skill: Content Gap Analysis (`/comp content`)

## Purpose
Identify content opportunities by systematically comparing your content footprint against competitors. A content gap analysis reveals topics competitors rank for that you do not, content formats they leverage that you have not explored, and audience segments they reach through content that you are missing. The goal is not to copy competitors but to find underserved topics where you can create superior, differentiated content that captures organic traffic, builds authority, and generates demand.

## When to Use
- Planning a quarterly or annual content strategy and need data-driven topic selection
- Organic traffic has plateaued and you need new growth vectors
- A competitor is outranking you on important keywords and you need to understand why
- Building a content moat around your core topics before competitors do
- Evaluating whether to invest in a new content format (video, podcast, tools, templates)
- Justifying content investment to stakeholders with competitive benchmarks

## Framework: 5-Layer Content Gap Analysis

### Layer 1: Topic Gap Analysis
Identify topics where competitors have ranking content but you do not.

**Process:**
1. List competitor domains (3-5 primary competitors)
2. For each competitor, identify their top 50-100 ranking keywords (using SEMrush, Ahrefs, or similar)
3. Cross-reference against your own ranking keywords
4. Categorize gaps into:
   - **Critical Gaps**: High-volume, high-intent topics where competitors rank and you don't
   - **Opportunity Gaps**: Medium-volume topics with low competition that you could win quickly
   - **Awareness Gaps**: Top-of-funnel topics that drive brand visibility
   - **Long-Tail Gaps**: Specific queries with clear intent that indicate product-market fit

Build a topic gap matrix:
```
| Topic/Keyword | Monthly Volume | Difficulty | Competitor A | Competitor B | You | Priority |
|---------------|---------------|------------|-------------|-------------|-----|----------|
| [topic] | [vol] | [1-100] | #[rank] | #[rank] | -- | Critical |
```

### Layer 2: Content Volume & Cadence Comparison
Compare content production metrics across competitors:

```
| Metric | You | Comp A | Comp B | Comp C |
|--------|-----|--------|--------|--------|
| Total blog posts | | | | |
| Posts per month (last 6mo) | | | | |
| Average word count | | | | |
| Content types (blog, video, etc.) | | | | |
| Content team size (estimated) | | | | |
| Guest/contributed content % | | | | |
```

Analyze cadence patterns:
- Are they accelerating or decelerating content production?
- Do they publish on a consistent schedule or in bursts?
- Is there a seasonal pattern to their content?
- How much of their content is evergreen vs time-sensitive?

### Layer 3: Content Quality & Depth Assessment
For the top 10-20 competing pieces of content, evaluate:
- **Depth Score**: Word count, heading structure, subtopics covered, examples included
- **Freshness**: Last updated date, accuracy of information, outdated sections
- **Media Richness**: Images, diagrams, videos, interactive elements, downloadable resources
- **E-E-A-T Signals**: Author expertise, citations, original research, first-hand experience
- **User Experience**: Page speed, readability, mobile optimization, ad density
- **Engagement**: Comments, social shares, backlinks earned

Rate each on a 1-5 scale:
```
| Content Piece | Depth | Freshness | Media | E-E-A-T | UX | Engagement | Overall |
|---------------|-------|-----------|-------|---------|----|-----------:|---------|
```

### Layer 4: Backlink Profile Comparison
Compare the link authority of competing content:
- **Domain Authority/Rating**: Overall site authority comparison
- **Page-Level Authority**: Specific page authority for competing content
- **Backlink Volume**: Total backlinks to competing pages
- **Referring Domain Quality**: Quality distribution of linking domains
- **Link Velocity**: Rate of new backlinks acquisition over time
- **Content-Driven Links**: Which content types earn the most links? (research, tools, guides, infographics)

### Layer 5: SERP Feature & Overlap Analysis
Analyze search engine results page dynamics:
- **SERP Overlap Percentage**: How often do you and competitors appear on the same SERP?
- **Featured Snippet Ownership**: Who holds featured snippets for key queries?
- **People Also Ask**: What related questions appear and who answers them?
- **SERP Feature Distribution**: Knowledge panels, local packs, video carousels, image packs
- **AI Overview Presence**: Are competitors cited in Google AI Overviews for key queries?
- **Brand vs Non-Brand**: Competitor organic visibility for branded vs non-branded terms

## Content Gap Prioritization Matrix
Score each gap on two axes to prioritize:

**Impact Score (1-5):**
- Search volume and traffic potential
- Commercial intent (closeness to purchase decision)
- Alignment with your product/service
- Brand authority building potential

**Feasibility Score (1-5):**
- Current keyword difficulty
- Existing content or expertise to leverage
- Resource requirements (writing, design, development)
- Time to rank (competition and domain authority gap)

Plot gaps on a 2x2: High Impact + High Feasibility = Immediate Priority

## Best Practices
1. Focus on intent, not just volume. A 200-search-per-month keyword with buying intent is more valuable than a 10,000-search keyword with informational intent if you are trying to drive revenue.
2. Do not just replicate competitor content — analyze what they missed, what's outdated, and where you can add unique value from first-hand experience.
3. Look at content formats, not just topics. If all competitors write blog posts but nobody has built an interactive tool or calculator, that is a format gap worth exploiting.
4. Update your gap analysis quarterly. Content landscapes shift as competitors publish new material and search algorithms evolve.
5. Cross-reference content gaps with your product roadmap. The best content strategy reinforces product positioning.
6. Track AI Overview citations — this is an increasingly important content gap dimension as search evolves toward AI-generated answers.
