# Google Ads Copy Policies — Quick Reference

## Editorial Policies That Affect Ad Copy

### Capitalization
- Title Case is allowed in headlines
- ALL CAPS is NOT allowed (except for acronyms: USA, HVAC, SEO)
- Random capitalization is not allowed: "bEsT pLuMbEr"
- Acceptable: "Best Plumber in Dallas" or "HVAC Repair Experts"

### Punctuation
- Only ONE exclamation mark per ad (and NOT in headlines)
- No repeated punctuation: "!!!" or "???" are disapproved
- Ellipsis (...) is generally disapproved
- Ampersand (&) is allowed and recommended to save characters
- Pipes (|) and bullets are not allowed in ad text

### Symbols & Characters
- Trademark symbols (TM, R, C) are allowed but count as characters
- Emojis are NOT allowed in Search ads (allowed in some Display/PMax)
- Phone numbers in ad text are NOT allowed (use call extensions instead)
- Email addresses in ad text are not recommended

### Spacing
- No extra spaces between words
- No spaces before punctuation
- Normal sentence spacing required

### Grammar & Spelling
- Intentional misspellings are disapproved
- Slang is generally allowed if professional
- Abbreviations are allowed (apt, info, govt)

## Content Restrictions

### Superlative Claims
- "Best", "#1", "Top Rated" require third-party verification
- Must be able to prove the claim if challenged
- Safer alternatives: "Award-Winning", "Highly Rated", "Top-Reviewed"
- "Leading" is acceptable with proof of market position

### Price & Offer Claims
- Prices in ads must match landing page prices
- "Free" claims must be genuinely free (no hidden costs)
- Sale/discount prices must be real discounts from regular prices
- Expiration dates in countdown ads must be accurate

### Competitor Mentions
- You CAN bid on competitor brand names as keywords
- You CANNOT use competitor trademarks in ad text (headlines/descriptions)
- You CAN say "Alternative to [competitor]" if competitor is not trademarked
- Comparison claims must be verifiable

### Restricted Industries
These industries have additional ad copy restrictions:
- **Healthcare**: No guarantees of outcomes, no before/after claims without proof
- **Financial**: Must include disclaimers, no guaranteed returns
- **Legal**: Varies by jurisdiction, no guaranteed outcomes
- **Alcohol**: Cannot promote excessive consumption
- **Gambling**: Restricted in many regions
- **Weight loss**: No unrealistic claims

## Landing Page Alignment Rules

- Ad claims MUST be supported on the landing page
- Offers in the ad MUST be visible on the landing page
- CTAs should match the landing page action (don't say "Call Now" if page has a form)
- Price mentioned in ad must match price on page
- Ad destination must be functional (no broken pages, no under-construction)

## Common Disapproval Reasons

1. **Misleading content** — Ad promises something the landing page doesn't deliver
2. **Unsubstantiated claims** — "Best" or "#1" without proof
3. **Excessive capitalization** — ALL CAPS words (except acronyms)
4. **Punctuation violations** — Multiple exclamation marks
5. **Destination mismatch** — Ad and landing page don't align
6. **Restricted content** — Industry-specific violations
7. **Trademark infringement** — Using competitor brand names in ad text
8. **Phone numbers in text** — Use call extensions instead
