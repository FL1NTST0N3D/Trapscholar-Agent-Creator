# Performance Max (PMax) Copy Guide

## PMax Text Assets

Performance Max campaigns use a different asset structure than Search RSAs.

### Asset Requirements

| Asset | Limit | Max Characters | Min Required |
|-------|-------|---------------|-------------|
| Headlines | 15 | 30 | 3 |
| Long Headlines | 5 | 90 | 1 |
| Descriptions | 5 | 90 | 2 |
| Short Descriptions | 5 | 60 | 1 (some formats) |
| Business Name | 1 | 25 | 1 |
| Call to Action | 1 | — | 1 (selected from list) |

### Long Headlines (PMax Exclusive)

Long headlines appear in Display, YouTube, Discovery, and Gmail placements. They have 90 characters — 3x more than Search headlines.

**Writing Long Headlines:**
- Use the full 90 characters — these have more space to work with
- Include a benefit + proof + CTA in one line
- Write them as standalone statements (they show alone on Display)
- Make them work visually on YouTube thumbnails and Gmail cards

**Long Headline Formulas:**
```
"[Benefit] — [Proof Point]. [CTA with offer]."
"[Problem]? [Solution] with [Brand]. [Trust signal]."
"[Specific result] in [timeframe]. Join [number]+ [customers/users]. [CTA]."
```

**Examples (90 chars max):**
- "Cut your energy bill by 40% with solar panels. Trusted by 15,000+ homeowners. Get a quote." (90)
- "Stop wasting money on ads that don't convert. AI-powered optimization from $99/mo. Try free." (90)
- "Handcrafted leather bags that last a lifetime. Rated 4.9/5 by 8,000+ buyers. Shop new arrivals." (95 - too long!)
- "Handcrafted leather bags for life. 4.9/5 from 8,000+ buyers. Shop new arrivals now." (84 - good!)

### Short Descriptions

Some PMax placements (especially Display) use short descriptions (60 chars).

**Writing Short Descriptions:**
- Focus on ONE key benefit or offer
- Include a CTA verb
- Think of them as extended headlines

**Examples:**
- "Save 30% on solar installation. Free consultation included." (59)
- "AI-powered ad optimization from $99/mo. Start your free trial." (62 - trim!)
- "AI ad optimization from $99/mo. Start your free trial today." (60)

### PMax Call-to-Action Options

PMax provides a fixed list of CTA buttons:
- Automated (Google chooses)
- Learn More
- Get Quote
- Apply Now
- Sign Up
- Contact Us
- Subscribe
- Download
- Book Now
- Shop Now
- Buy Now
- Get Offer
- Order Now
- See More

**Recommendation:** Use "Automated" unless you have a strong reason to lock a specific CTA. Google's algorithm picks the best CTA per placement and audience.

## PMax vs Search — Copy Differences

| Aspect | Search RSA | PMax |
|--------|-----------|------|
| Headlines | 30 chars, 15 max | 30 chars, 15 max + Long Headlines (90 chars, 5 max) |
| Descriptions | 90 chars, 4 max | 90 chars, 5 max + Short Descriptions (60 chars) |
| Context | User actively searching | Mixed: Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, Maps |
| Intent | High (keyword-based) | Mixed (audience + signal-based) |
| CTA | In ad text | Separate CTA button + ad text |
| Tone | Direct, search-intent-focused | Broader, works across placements |

### Writing for Mixed Placements

PMax copy must work across:
- **Search**: User is actively looking — benefit + CTA
- **Display**: User is browsing — attention-grabbing + benefit
- **YouTube**: User is watching — curiosity + benefit
- **Gmail**: User is in inbox — offer + CTA
- **Maps**: User is nearby — local relevance + CTA

Write copy that makes sense in ALL contexts. Avoid search-only language like "Search results show..." or display-only language like "Click the banner..."

## PMax Asset Group Strategy

Each asset group in PMax can have its own text assets. Structure asset groups by:
- Product category (shoes, shirts, accessories)
- Audience segment (new customers, returners, lookalikes)
- Goal (prospecting, remarketing, brand)

Each asset group should have:
- Headlines tailored to that specific product/audience
- Descriptions emphasizing relevant USPs
- Long headlines that tell the complete story for that segment
- Shared brand elements (business name, trust signals)

## PMax Copy Best Practices

1. **Maximize assets** — provide all 15 headlines, 5 long headlines, 5 descriptions
2. **Write for the uninitiated** — PMax reaches people who may not know your brand
3. **Include brand name** in at least 2 headlines
4. **Use long headlines as mini-pitches** — 90 chars = a complete value proposition
5. **Test different emotional angles** across headlines for different placements
6. **Update seasonally** — PMax copy should reflect current offers and seasons
7. **Don't duplicate Search RSA copy** — PMax audiences are different, write accordingly
