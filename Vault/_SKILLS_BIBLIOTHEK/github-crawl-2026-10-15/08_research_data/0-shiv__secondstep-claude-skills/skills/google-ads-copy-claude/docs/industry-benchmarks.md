## Headline Performance Patterns

### What Works (Across Industries)

1. **Specific numbers outperform vague claims**
   - "Save 42%" > "Save Big" (+36% CTR)
   - "In 24 Hours" > "Fast" (+28% CTR)

2. **Questions outperform statements for high-intent keywords**
   - "Need a Plumber?" > "Professional Plumbing" (+18% CTR)

3. **Benefit headlines outperform feature headlines**
   - "Sleep Better Tonight" > "Memory Foam Mattress" (+22% CTR)

4. **Short headlines (18-24 chars) often outperform maxed-out headlines**
   - More readable, stronger emphasis per word

5. **Social proof headlines work best in position 2 or 3**
   - "Rated #1 by Consumers" works better when paired with a benefit headline in H1

### What Doesn't Work

1. **Generic superlatives without proof**: "Best Service Ever" (-15% CTR vs specific claims)
2. **Feature-only messaging**: "We Have 500 Products" (no benefit stated)
3. **ALL CAPS or excessive punctuation**: Gets disapproved and looks spammy
4. **Same CTA in every headline**: Kills Ad Strength and wastes headline slots

## Description Performance Patterns

1. **Descriptions that start with a benefit get +12% CTR** vs those starting with features
2. **Descriptions ending with a CTA get +8% conversion rate** vs those without
3. **90-character descriptions get +5% CTR** vs 60-70 character descriptions (more visible)
4. **Descriptions with 2-3 USPs outperform single-USP descriptions** (+15% CTR)

## Quality Score Relationship

Ad copy directly affects Quality Score through Expected CTR:

| Quality Score | CPC Discount | Impression Share |
|--------------|-------------|-----------------|
| 10 | -50% CPC | Maximum |
| 8-9 | -20-30% CPC | High |
| 7 | Baseline | Average |
| 5-6 | +25-50% CPC | Below average |
| 1-4 | +100-400% CPC | Minimal |

Better ad copy = higher CTR = better Quality Score = lower CPCs = more conversions for the same budget.
