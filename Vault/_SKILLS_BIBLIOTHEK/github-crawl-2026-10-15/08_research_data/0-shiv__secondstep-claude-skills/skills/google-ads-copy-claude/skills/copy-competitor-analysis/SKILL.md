# Competitor Analysis — Ad Copy Intelligence & Differentiation

## Command: `/copy competitor`

Analyze competitor ad copy patterns from Google Ads auction data, identify messaging gaps, and build differentiation strategies that make your ads stand out.

## Required Inputs

- **Your business**: Name, product/service, USPs
- **Primary keywords**: 3-5 keywords you're bidding on
- **Competitor ads** (provide any available): Headlines, descriptions, URLs seen in auction
- **Competitor names** (if known): Direct competitors in your space
- **Industry**: Category for benchmark comparisons

If the user doesn't have competitor ad data, guide them to these sources:
1. **Google Ads Auction Insights** — shows who you compete against
2. **Google search** — manually search your keywords and screenshot competitor ads
3. **SpyFu / SEMrush / iSpionage** — competitor ad history tools
4. **Google Ads Transparency Center** — ads.google.com/transparency

## Analysis Framework

### Step 1: Competitor Ad Copy Inventory

Organize competitor copy into a structured table:

```
=== COMPETITOR AD INVENTORY ===

COMPETITOR A: [Name]
  Headlines observed:
    - "headline 1"
    - "headline 2"
    ...
  Descriptions observed:
    - "description 1"
    ...
  Extensions seen: [sitelinks, callouts, etc.]
  Landing page: [URL]

COMPETITOR B: [Name]
  [same structure]
```

### Step 2: Pattern Extraction

Identify patterns across all competitor ads:

**Messaging Themes** — What topics do competitors emphasize?
- Price/value (e.g., "Lowest Price Guaranteed", "Save 30%")
- Quality/expertise (e.g., "Award-Winning", "20 Years Experience")
- Speed/convenience (e.g., "Same-Day Delivery", "Book in 60 Seconds")
- Trust/safety (e.g., "Licensed & Insured", "Money-Back Guarantee")
- Selection/variety (e.g., "1,000+ Options", "Custom Solutions")

**CTA Patterns** — What actions do competitors push?
- "Get a Free Quote" vs "Call Now" vs "Shop Now" vs "Learn More"
- Which CTAs are overused? Which are missing?

**Emotional Triggers** — What emotions are competitors targeting?
- Fear of missing out (urgency, scarcity)
- Trust and security (guarantees, credentials)
- Aspiration (transformation, results)
- Convenience (ease, speed, simplicity)

**Structural Patterns** — How are competitor ads formatted?
- DKI usage (do they use dynamic keyword insertion?)
- Number usage (stats, prices, percentages)
- Question vs statement headlines
- Brand name positioning (H1 or not?)

### Step 3: Gap Analysis

The most valuable part — find what competitors are NOT saying:

```
=== GAP ANALYSIS ===

OVERUSED THEMES (competitors all say this):
  - "Free Quote" — 4 of 5 competitors use this exact CTA
  - "Years of Experience" — 3 of 5 lead with longevity
  - Generic quality claims — "Best", "Top", "Premium"

UNDERUSED THEMES (opportunity zones):
  - Speed/turnaround — no competitor mentions delivery time
  - Specific results — no competitor uses concrete numbers
  - Technology — no competitor mentions modern tools/tech
  - Guarantee — only 1 competitor offers a guarantee
  - Social proof — no competitor mentions review count or rating

MISSING ENTIRELY:
  - No competitor addresses [specific pain point]
  - No competitor mentions [specific feature]
  - No competitor targets [specific audience segment]
```

### Step 4: Differentiation Strategy

Build ad copy that fills the gaps and stands out:

**Differentiation Principles:**

1. **Say what they don't** — If every competitor says "Free Quote", lead with speed: "Quote in 60 Seconds"
2. **Be specific where they're generic** — They say "Experienced Team", you say "127 Projects Completed"
3. **Address the unspoken objection** — They ignore pricing concerns, you say "No Hidden Fees — Ever"
4. **Use a different emotional angle** — They use trust, you use aspiration: "Your Dream Kitchen Awaits"
5. **Own a format they don't** — They use statements, you use questions: "Still Waiting for Repairs?"

### Step 5: Competitive Copy Generation

Generate headlines and descriptions that directly exploit identified gaps:

```
=== DIFFERENTIATED AD COPY ===

GAP: No competitor mentions speed
  H1: "Done in 24 Hours or Free" (24 chars) [Differentiation: Speed]
  H2: "Fastest Turnaround in TX" (24 chars) [Differentiation: Speed]

GAP: No competitor uses specific numbers
  H3: "127 Projects Completed" (22 chars) [Differentiation: Proof]
  H4: "98.5% Client Retention" (22 chars) [Differentiation: Proof]

GAP: Competitors all use "Free Quote" CTA
  H5: "Instant Online Pricing" (22 chars) [Differentiation: CTA]
  H6: "See Pricing — No Calls" (22 chars) [Differentiation: CTA]
```

## Competitive Positioning Strategies

### Strategy 1: The Counter-Position
When all competitors cluster around one message, go the opposite direction.
- Competitors: "Cheapest Price" → You: "Premium Quality, Fair Price"
- Competitors: "Fast and Easy" → You: "Thorough and Precise"
- Competitors: "Big Selection" → You: "Curated, Expert-Picked"

### Strategy 2: The Specificity Play
Replace vague competitor claims with hyper-specific details.
- Competitor: "Great Customer Service" → You: "Average Response: 4 Minutes"
- Competitor: "Top Rated" → You: "4.9/5 Across 2,847 Reviews"
- Competitor: "Affordable" → You: "Plans From $29/Month"

### Strategy 3: The Risk Reversal
Address the purchase anxiety that competitors ignore.
- "Full Refund if Not Satisfied"
- "No Long-Term Contracts"
- "Try Free for 30 Days"
- "Pay Only for Results"

### Strategy 4: The Authority Stack
Combine multiple proof points that competitors can't match.
- "[Certification] + [Years] + [Number of Clients]"
- "Board-Certified | 15 Years | 5,000+ Patients"

### Strategy 5: The Problem Ownership
Own a specific problem that competitors don't mention.
- Competitor talks about: their product features
- You talk about: the customer's specific frustration
- "Tired of [specific thing competitors don't address]?"

## When Competitor Data Is Limited

If the user has little or no competitor data:
1. Search the primary keywords yourself (if web access available) and note ad patterns
2. Use industry benchmarks to predict common competitor messaging
3. Focus on making the user's USPs the center of differentiation
4. Recommend the user run a manual competitive audit using the checklist above
5. Generate copy based on the user's unique strengths rather than competitive positioning
