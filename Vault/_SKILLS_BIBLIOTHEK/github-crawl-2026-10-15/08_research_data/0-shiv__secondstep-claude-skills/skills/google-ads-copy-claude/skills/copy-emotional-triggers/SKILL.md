# Emotional Triggers — Power Words & Emotional Scoring for Google Ads

## Command: `/copy emotional`

Score ad copy for emotional impact using a database of 200+ power words, categorized by emotion. Generate emotionally charged headline and description variations.

## Why Emotional Triggers Matter

Ads that trigger an emotional response get 2-3x higher CTR than purely rational ads. Even in B2B, decision-makers are human — emotion drives clicks, logic justifies them.

The key is using the RIGHT emotion for the RIGHT audience at the RIGHT stage of the funnel.

## Emotional Scoring System

Score each headline and description for emotional impact:

### Scoring Criteria

| Score | Level | Criteria |
|-------|-------|---------|
| 0-2 | Flat | No power words, purely informational |
| 3-4 | Mild | 1 power word, low emotional charge |
| 5-6 | Moderate | 1-2 power words, clear emotional angle |
| 7-8 | Strong | 2+ power words, strong emotional pull |
| 9-10 | Maximum | Multiple power words, vivid emotional imagery |

### Example Scoring

```
"Professional Tax Services" → Score: 1/10 (flat, no emotion)
"Save Thousands on Taxes" → Score: 6/10 (greed: save, thousands)
"Don't Overpay Taxes Again" → Score: 7/10 (fear: don't, overpay + urgency: again)
"Stop Wasting Money on Taxes" → Score: 8/10 (fear: stop, wasting + greed: money)
```
