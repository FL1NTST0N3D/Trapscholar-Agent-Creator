# CTA Optimizer — Call-to-Action Analysis by Industry & Goal

## Command: `/copy cta`

Analyze, recommend, and A/B test CTAs based on industry, campaign goal, funnel stage, and device. Includes performance benchmarks and CTA formulas.

## Required Inputs

- **Industry**: The business vertical
- **Campaign goal**: Leads, sales, calls, sign-ups, downloads, bookings
- **Funnel stage**: Top (awareness), Middle (consideration), Bottom (decision)
- **Device split**: Primarily mobile, desktop, or mixed
- **Current CTAs** (if auditing): What CTAs are currently in use

## CTA Formula Framework

### Formula 1: [Action Verb] + [Benefit]
Strongest formula. Combines what to do with why.
- "Save 30% Today"
- "Get Faster Results"
- "Start Growing Now"

### Formula 2: [Action Verb] + [Object] + [Free/Now]
Classic direct response. Clear, specific, urgent.
- "Get Your Free Quote Now"
- "Download the Free Guide"
- "Book Your Free Consult"

### Formula 3: [Benefit] — [Action Verb]
Lead with the benefit, close with the action.
- "Better Sleep — Shop Now"
- "More Leads — Get Started"
- "Lower Bills — Switch Today"

### Formula 4: [Urgency] + [Action Verb]
Time pressure drives immediate action.
- "Limited Time — Order Now"
- "Today Only — Claim Yours"
- "Ending Soon — Shop Deals"

### Formula 5: [Social Proof] + [Action Verb]
Combine trust with action for high-intent audiences.
- "Join 50,000+ Users"
- "See Why We're #1 Rated"
- "Try the Award-Winner"

## CTA Optimization by Device

### Mobile CTAs
Mobile users are action-oriented. Use short, tap-friendly CTAs:
- "Call Now" (with click-to-call extension)
- "Get Directions" (with location extension)
- "Text Us"
- "Book Now"
- Mobile users convert 2x more on CTAs with 2-3 words vs 5+ words

### Desktop CTAs
Desktop users are research-oriented. Use informational CTAs:
- "Compare Plans & Pricing"
- "See Full Product Details"
- "Schedule a Demo"
- "Read Customer Reviews"
- Desktop users engage more with CTAs that promise information vs immediate purchase

## CTA A/B Testing Framework

### Test Structure
Always test CTAs in pairs with a clear hypothesis:

```
TEST: [Test Name]
HYPOTHESIS: "[CTA A] will outperform [CTA B] because [reason]"

VARIANT A (Control): "Get a Free Quote"
VARIANT B (Challenger): "See Pricing Instantly"

METRICS TO TRACK:
  - CTR (primary)
  - Conversion rate (primary)
  - Cost per conversion (secondary)
  - Quality Score impact (secondary)

MINIMUM SAMPLE: 1,000 impressions per variant
MINIMUM DURATION: 14 days
CONFIDENCE LEVEL: 95%
```

### What to Test

| Test Priority | What to Change | Example |
|--------------|---------------|---------|
| 1 (High) | CTA verb | "Get" vs "Start" vs "Try" |
| 2 (High) | Free vs paid framing | "Free Quote" vs "See Pricing" |
| 3 (Medium) | Urgency modifier | "Shop Now" vs "Shop Today" vs "Shop" |
| 4 (Medium) | Specificity | "Learn More" vs "See 5-Star Reviews" |
| 5 (Low) | Word order | "Get Your Free Quote" vs "Free Quote — Get Yours" |

## CTA Audit Checklist

When auditing existing CTAs:

- [ ] Is the CTA relevant to the landing page action? (Don't say "Call Now" if the page has a form)
- [ ] Does the CTA match the funnel stage? (Don't say "Buy Now" for awareness campaigns)
- [ ] Is the CTA specific? ("Get Your Free Roof Inspection" > "Learn More")
- [ ] Are there at least 2 different CTAs across headlines and descriptions?
- [ ] Does the mobile CTA align with mobile user behavior?
- [ ] Is there urgency when appropriate? ("Today", "Now", "Before Friday")
- [ ] Does the CTA promise a benefit? ("Save 30%" > "Submit")
- [ ] Is the commitment level appropriate? (Free trial > Paid commitment for cold traffic)
