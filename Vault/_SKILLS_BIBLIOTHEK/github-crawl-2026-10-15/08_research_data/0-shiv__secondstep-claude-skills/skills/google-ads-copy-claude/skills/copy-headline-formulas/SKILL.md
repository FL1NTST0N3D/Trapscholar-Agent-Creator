# Headline Formulas — 50+ Proven Google Ads Headline Templates

## Command: `/copy headlines`

Generate headline variations using 50+ proven formulas, categorized by type. Each formula includes the template, an example, and category tags for easy filtering.

## Required Inputs

- **Business/Product**: What is being advertised
- **Primary Keyword**: The search term this ad targets
- **USP**: Key differentiator (price, speed, quality, guarantee)
- **Numbers available**: Stats, years in business, customer count, ratings, prices
- **Location** (if applicable): City, state, region

## How to Use These Formulas

### Step 1: Select the user's goal
Map the campaign objective to the best formula categories:
- **Brand awareness** → Social Proof + Benefit formulas
- **Lead generation** → Question + CTA + Urgency formulas
- **E-commerce sales** → Price/Offer + Urgency + Comparison formulas
- **Local services** → Local + Social Proof + CTA formulas

### Step 2: Generate variations
Take 8-10 relevant formulas and fill them in with the user's specific business details. Count characters carefully — every headline must be 30 characters or fewer.

### Step 3: Check uniqueness
No two headlines should say the same thing in different words. Google's Ad Strength algorithm detects semantic similarity and penalizes it.

### Step 4: Tag and categorize
Tag each headline with its formula category so the user can ensure variety across their RSA.

## Pro Tips

- Headlines with numbers get 36% higher CTR on average
- Question headlines work best for high-intent, specific keywords
- Urgency headlines perform best in remarketing and seasonal campaigns
- Local formulas are essential for service-area businesses
- Always include at least 1 headline with the exact primary keyword
- Mix short (15-20 char) headlines with long (28-30 char) headlines for more combinations
