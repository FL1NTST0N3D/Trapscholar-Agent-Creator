Welcome to Weedtree, the family tree of cannabis strains

With the massive variability in effects, I’ve always been curious about how cannabis strain lineages relate, particularly between the strains I prefer. So I built the lineage chart I couldn’t find elsewhere.

WeedTree is a work in progress, compiled from many international sources that often disagree, particularly around older or proprietary cultivars. I’ve made practical simplifications around cultivars, phenotypes, clones, variants, renaming, lost breeding history, and disputed pedigrees so the information can be represented as a navigable family tree. The chart now covers a substantial range of historical and modern cannabis lineages, but gaps, uncertainties, and errors will inevitably remain.

Ancestor contribution percentages are calculated from the relationships recorded in the chart. Where explicit parent weightings are known, those weightings are used; otherwise recorded parents are treated as equal contributors. These percentages are useful for exploring lineage, but should not be interpreted as measured genetic composition. They cannot account for selective breeding, gene expression, phenotype selection, genetic recombination, undocumented backcrossing, or inaccuracies in the underlying pedigree information.

Node colours indicate the strain’s broad indica–sativa dominance, with the displayed percentage calculated from the lineage recorded in the chart. These percentages are intended as a visual guide to ancestry and should not be treated as measured botanical or genetic composition.

Please enjoy exploring the lineages and the patterns they make. Click on strains for more lineage details.
