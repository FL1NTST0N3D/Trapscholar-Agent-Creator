Scientific anchors

Opinionated about seven points commonly mis-stated in operator-level material — healthy leaf temp is 1–3 °C BELOW air; pore EC drifts via two mechanisms not one; light saturation is conditional on CO2; cannabis stomata begin anticipatory closure around leaf-VPD 1.5–1.6 kPa; botrytis needs sustained RH >93–95% or free water; F-02 explains how a "perfect" room sensor coexists with a sick canopy; a refrigerant dehumidifier is a net heater.
